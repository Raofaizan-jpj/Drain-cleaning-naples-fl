What Makes the Price Go Up or Down?

The price of drain service changes based on the equipment required, the severity of the obstruction, and pipe accessibility. A simple branch line clog takes less time than clearing heavy buildup out of an exterior line.

When a technician can access an outdoor cleanout at ground level, labor time remains low. If equipment must be carried onto a roof or a toilet must be pulled to reach the line, costs rise. Clearing soft soap residue with [LINK TO DRAIN SNAKING AND CABLING] is less labor-intensive than removing dense root masses from older clay lines.

Does Snaking or Hydro Jetting Cost More?

Hydro jetting costs more than snaking because it uses specialized trailer-mounted machinery and high-pressure water to scrub the entire interior pipe wall. A motorized snake cuts a pathway through soft clogs, but jetting cleans out accumulated grease, sand, and scale.

Snaking is often the first option for localized branch clogs, such as an individual toilet or shower stoppage. For recurring problems or lines packed with heavy grease, [LINK TO HYDRO JETTING] scours pipe walls back to full diameter, helping prevent repeat visits.

When Can a Simple Job Become a Bigger One?

A simple clog becomes a larger job when the camera discovers structural pipe defects, root intrusion, or pipe collapse rather than a basic stoppage. In those situations, running a snake only provides temporary relief.

Older homes in the area often have aging cast iron lines with bottom channeling or cracked joints. When a pipe wall is broken, standard clearing cannot fix the issue, and the line requires [LINK TO DRAIN PIPE REPAIR] to prevent wastewater leaks under the foundation slab.

Naples drainage systems deal with subtropical storm sand, mineral scale from hard water, and aggressive ficus and palm roots. Because many local homes sit on a concrete slab, a main line backup quickly surfaces in the lowest household fixture.

Collier County Public Utilities reports that fats, oil, and grease cause most sewer blockages across the county. Flushed wipes and grease stick to mineral scale inside older pipes, causing repeated backups. How Can I Avoid Paying Twice for the Same Clog?

You can avoid paying twice by verifying that the line is clear with a second camera pass and running water after the work is complete. Clearing only the center of a clog leaves buildup behind that catches paper and causes another backup weeks later.

After we complete our work, we run water through the fixtures to test the flow. On main sewer jobs, we put the camera back down the pipe so you can see that the line is fully open before our team leaves.

Drain Cleaning Cost FAQs

How much does a basic drain cleaning cost in Florida?

Published guides in Florida show typical drain cleaning costs ranging from $190 to over $500, depending on the clog location and equipment used.

Why is there no fixed price online for every Naples drain job?

No two clogs are identical; a blockage caused by tree roots in an underground main line requires different equipment and labor than hair trapped in a bathroom sink.

Is a video camera inspection included in the quote?

We inspect the line with an HD camera first to identify the problem and establish a firm, honest price before starting any clearing work.

Does hydro jetting cost more than mechanical drain snaking?

Yes. Hydro jetting uses specialized high-pressure water machinery that scrubs the pipe walls down to bare surface, which costs more than running a cable snake.

Why do main sewer lines back up in Naples slab homes?

Slab homes have plumbing running beneath concrete, meaning a main line stoppage from tree roots, grease, or storm sand forces wastewater up through the lowest drains, such as showers and tubs.
