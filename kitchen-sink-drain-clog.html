What causes kitchen sink clogs in Naples?

Kitchen sink clogs in Naples are often caused by grease buildup, food particles, and foreign objects. Regular cleaning can prevent these issues from escalating.

How to prevent kitchen sink clogs in Naples?

To prevent kitchen sink clogs in Naples, avoid pouring grease down the drain, use a strainer to catch food debris, and clear the trap if flow slows down.

How much does kitchen sink drain clearing cost in Naples?

Pricing is based on whether the blockage is in the trap, disposal connection, or deeper branch line. We inspect the line and quote the price before work starts.

How to unclog a kitchen sink in Naples?

To unclog a kitchen sink in Naples, you can try using a plunger or a hand snake. For persistent clogs affecting multiple fixtures, explore our broader clogged drain clearing or check your dishwasher connection for a dishwasher air gap leak.

What does a kitchen sink drain cleaning service include in Naples?

A visit covers checking the trap, clearing grease or food blockages with a sink cable, and testing flow. You hear the price before work starts.

Why is my kitchen sink backing up into the other side?

A double sink shares a common branch line. When grease or food blocks the pipe past the tee fitting, water backs up into the other basin. Clearing the branch line restores flow.
