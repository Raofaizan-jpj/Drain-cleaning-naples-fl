What is drain snaking in Naples, Florida?

Drain snaking in Naples, Florida is a plumbing technique that uses a flexible auger, commonly known as a drain snake, to remove clogs from pipes. This method is effective for clearing blockages caused by hair, grease, or foreign objects lodged in your plumbing system.

In Naples, hair, soap scum, grease, and flushed wipes cause many fixture clogs. A snake clears a clog at one fixture. If clogs keep coming back, or several drains slow down together, the problem may be in a larger line, and a look inside the pipe shows why. Floor drains and lanai drains can also fill with sand and leaves, and a cable can clear what sits just past the grate. For a toilet, a short toilet auger is the usual tool.

When Is a Snake Not Enough?

A snake is the right tool for one fixture. Some jobs need another fix.

Grease on the pipe wall

A snake opens a hole, but a coating of grease can stay on the pipe wall. High pressure water cleans it better.

Several drains slow at once

That points to the main line, not one fixture. A camera shows where the line is blocked.

Old or damaged pipe

A cable can catch on a cracked or broken joint. A camera check shows the pipe's condition before we push a cable through it.

What is drain snaking in Naples?

Drain snaking uses a flexible cable to break up and pull out a clog in a pipe. It works best on a clog at a single sink, tub, shower, or toilet.

How does drain snaking work in Naples?

Drain snaking works by inserting a specialized tool, the drain snake, into the pipe. It breaks up and removes blockages, restoring water flow.

When should I use drain snaking in Naples?

Use drain snaking when experiencing isolated slow sinks, tubs, or branch line clogs. For extensive scale, sand, or tree root blockages in main sewer pipes, we recommend high pressure hydro jetting or full sewer line cleaning.
