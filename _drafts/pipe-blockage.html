What causes pipe blockages in Naples?

Common causes of pipe blockages in Naples include tree root intrusion, grease buildup, and debris accumulation. These factors can lead to slow drains and sewage backups if not addressed promptly.

How much does pipe blockage repair cost in Naples?

Typically, pipe blockage repair in Naples costs between $180 and $450, depending on the severity of the blockage and the methods used. For accurate pricing, an on-site inspection is recommended.

When should I call for pipe blockage repair in Naples?

You should call for pipe blockage repair in Naples if you notice slow drains, gurgling sounds, or sewage backups. These are signs that immediate attention is needed to prevent further damage.

How can I prevent pipe blockages in Naples?

To prevent pipe blockages, avoid disposing of grease and large food particles down the drain, regularly clean your drains, and consider periodic professional inspections to catch issues early.
