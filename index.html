Why Drains Clog in Naples

Most backups here trace back to a few causes. We find which one is in your line before we suggest a fix.

01

Roots

Ficus and palm roots push into joints in older clay and cast iron pipe. Big trees near the house are a common cause in older neighborhoods.

02

Grease and Wipes

Collier County Public Utilities says fats, oil, and grease cause most sewer blockages. Wipes sold as flushable do not break down like paper.

03

Scale, Sand, and Sags

Mineral scale narrows pipe walls over time. Storm runoff can wash sand into lines, and a sagging pipe holds waste in one spot.

Do I Have City Sewer or Septic in Naples?

Check your utility bill for a sewer charge. If you see one, you are on public sewer. If not, you may be on septic. Condo and HOA homes can pay sewer through the association, so ask yours. You can also call the City of Naples or Collier County Utilities. Knowing this first saves you from paying for the wrong fix.

01

Public Sewer

Waste flows to a public main. Who owns each part of the line depends on your utility, so ask before you pay for a dig.

02

Septic Tank

Waste goes to a buried tank and drain field. Some Naples neighborhoods, like Gulf Acres and Rosemary Heights, were on septic before the City added sewer lines.

03

Not Sure?

Do not pour drain chemicals or septic additives down the line. The EPA does not recommend septic tank additives.

What Happens to Naples Sewers During a Storm?

Heavy rain and storm surge can overload sewers and cut power to pump stations, which can push sewage back up into low drains. Use less water during a storm, and stop using water if it rises in a tub or shower. After the water drops, call a pro before you use the drains again.

01

Hurricane Irma, 2017

Collier County runs about 900 pump stations, and nearly all lost power. Sewage came up in some streets and homes.

02

Hurricane Ian, 2022

Flooding destroyed about 50 City of Naples sewer pumps, and some areas saw 8 to 10 feet of storm surge.

03

Before and After

Skip extra flushes, laundry, and the dishwasher during a storm. Afterward, sand and debris can pack a line. Ask your insurance agent about sewer backup coverage.

How much does drain cleaning cost in Naples?

The total cost depends on which line is backed up, how deep the obstruction sits, the condition of the piping, and the time of the call. We inspect the issue first, then give you a firm written quote. You authorize the work only after you have reviewed the numbers.

What makes Naples Drain Pros a good choice in Naples?

We run a pipe camera first, so you see the exact issue before you spend money on a fix. We select the tool that matches the blockage, go over the inspection, and confirm the estimate before starting. We are licensed and insured, and you can ask for proof.

What are the signs I need my drains cleaned?

Slow sinks or tubs, gurgling after a flush, a sewer smell from a floor drain, or water in the shower when the washer runs. If the same backup keeps returning, or several fixtures act up at once, call us. That often points to the main sewer line.

How do I schedule a visit?

Call us or send the quote form. Tell us which drain is affected, how long it has been slow, and whether you have sewer or septic. We confirm an arrival window, and a technician arrives ready to conduct a video inspection.

Is emergency service available?

Yes, we take urgent backup calls. If sewage is coming up, stop using water, keep children and pets away, and call right away. Do not pour chemicals down the drain. They will not clear a backup and can harm your pipes.

What is the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?

A snake breaks through a soft clog at one fixture. Jetting uses high pressure water to wash grease, sludge, and roots off the pipe wall, so it suits longer lines and repeat clogs. It is not safe for cracked or fragile old pipe, so we inspect the line with video equipment first.

How often should drains and sewer lines be cleaned?

Most homes only need service when a drain slows down. Older properties with mature ficus or palm roots benefit from a periodic video inspection. Restaurants should clean grease lines on a regular schedule, and we can set that up.

Are chemical drain cleaners safe for pipes?

No. University of Maryland Extension says drain cleaners contain some of the most corrosive chemicals in a home, including lye and strong acids, and they can corrode metal pipe. Try a plunger or hand snake first, then call us if the clog stays.

Why does my drain smell after I come back from being away for months?

A dry trap is the most common cause. The water in a trap blocks sewer gas, and it can dry out in a closed home. Run water in every drain for a minute. If the smell stays, or drains are slow or gurgle, call us for a look.

How do I keep lanai and pool deck drains clear?

Clear leaves, sand, and debris off the grate often, especially before the rainy season. Pour a bucket of water into the drain to check that it flows. If water pools or drains slowly, the line may be blocked, so call us.

Backed Up Right Now?

Standing water, a gurgling toilet, or a sewer smell means something is blocked. Call us and we will send a technician. We inspect the line on screen, explain why it stopped flowing, and give you an upfront estimate before any work begins.
