How much does drain cleaning cost in Naples?

The price depends on which drain is blocked, how far the clog sits, the pipe's condition, and the time of the call. We look at the problem first, then give you a fixed price. You decide on the work only after you have heard it.

What makes Drain Cleaning Advisor a good choice in Naples?

We put a camera in the pipe first, so you see the cause before you pay for a fix. We pick the tool that fits the clog, explain what we found, and set the price before any work begins. We are licensed and insured, and you can ask for proof.

What are the signs I need my drains cleaned?

Slow sinks or tubs, gurgling after a flush, a sewer smell from a floor drain, or water in the shower when the washer runs. If the same clog keeps coming back, or several drains act up at once, call us. That often points to the main line.

How do I schedule a visit?

Call us or send the quote form. Tell us which drain is affected, how long it has been slow, and whether you have sewer or septic. We confirm a time window, and a technician arrives ready to look with the camera.

Is emergency service available?

Yes, we take urgent backup calls. If sewage is coming up, stop using water, keep children and pets away, and call right away. Do not pour chemicals down the drain. They will not clear a backup and can harm your pipes.

What is the difference between snaking and hydro jetting?

A snake breaks through a soft clog at one fixture. Jetting uses high pressure water to wash grease, sludge, and roots off the pipe wall, so it suits longer lines and repeat clogs. It is not safe for cracked or fragile old pipe, so we check with the camera first.

How often should drains and sewer lines be cleaned?

Most homes only need service when a drain slows down. Older homes with large trees nearby benefit from a camera check from time to time. Restaurants should clean grease lines on a regular schedule, and we can set that up.

Are chemical drain cleaners safe for pipes?

No. University of Maryland Extension says drain cleaners contain some of the most corrosive chemicals in a home, including lye and strong acids, and they can corrode metal pipe. Try a plunger or hand snake first, then call us if the clog stays.

Need Immediate Drain Cleaning in Naples?

Standing water, a gurgling toilet, or a sewer smell means something is blocked. Call us and we will send a technician. We look inside the pipe with a camera, show you the cause, and quote a price before we touch a tool — our team is ready to work.
